# Data Contract & Execution Policy

This document serves as the single source of truth for metrics, definitions, and operational policies for the Trading System.

## 1. Metric Definitions
- **Signal**: An intent to enter or exit a position generated by a strategy engine, containing direction, asset, and timestamp.
- **Entry**: The execution of a signal that opens a new position or adds to an existing one.
- **Fill**: A confirmed execution from the exchange containing exact price, quantity, commission, and time.
- **Round Trip**: A complete sequence of opening and fully closing a position.
- **Trade / Day**: Measured as total portfolio entries within a rolling 24-hour window (00:00 - 23:59 UTC).
- **Fee**: The actual exchange commission paid (e.g., Binance VIP 0 Maker/Taker rates).
- **Funding**: Periodic payments exchanged between longs and shorts in Perpetual Futures.
- **Realized PnL**: Profit or loss locked in upon closing a position (including fees and funding).
- **Unrealized PnL**: Current floating profit or loss of open positions based on the mark price.
- **Equity**: Total Cash Balance + Unrealized PnL.
- **Drawdown**: The percentage decline from the peak equity curve to the lowest point before a new peak is achieved.
- **Exposure**: The total notional value of open positions.
- **Win/Loss**: A round trip is considered a Win if the Realized PnL (net of fees) is > 0, otherwise a Loss.

## 2. Hard Limits & Risk Budget
- **Max Portfolio Drawdown**: 20%. If reached, all trading is halted.
- **Trade Counting**: The limit of 1–10 trades/day is counted **separately** for the Spot engine and the Futures engine.
- **Capital Allocation**: Spot and Futures budgets are strictly separated. They do not share capital or margin.
- **Symbol Universe**: Fixed at 20 coins.

## 3. Operational Mechanics
- **Mode**: Futures will use Hedge Mode (or whichever is proven mathematically most profitable without causing state conflicts) to allow flexible positional play.
- **Cost Assumptions**: All simulations and evaluations must use **actual costs**, meaning 0.02% Maker / 0.05% Taker (or current VIP tier) + measured historical slippage + actual historical funding rates.

## 4. Promotion Pipeline
- **Approval Flow**: `Strategy Lab (Discovery)` -> `Validation (OOS / Replay)` -> `LIVE TRADING`.
- **Paper Trading**: Bypassed. Strategies go straight to live trading upon user approval.
- **Authority**: Only the system owner (the user) has the authority to approve, promote, or rollback a strategy. **NO AUTO PROMOTION or AUTO ROLLBACK** modifying the strategy logic is permitted without explicit manual approval.
